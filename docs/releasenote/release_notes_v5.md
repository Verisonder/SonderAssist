## SonderAssist 5

A second way the phone can tell it has been taken — one that works while it is asleep in
your pocket.

### The strap

Pick a device you wear. If it disconnects and does not come back, the phone decides it is
gone.

It happens **silently**. No message, no sound, and the screen is not woken — so from the
outside nothing has happened at all. Underneath, everything that follows a grab follows
this too: the phone locks, the location starts going out, and the power menu is closed.
The next time you turn the screen on the alert is waiting, and it has to be dismissed the
same way any other one is.

Unlike the grab detector, which only watches between unlocking and the screen going off,
this works with the screen already off and from the lock screen. That is the point of it:
a phone is most takeable when it is dark in a pocket.

- **One device, chosen by you**, matched on its hardware address rather than its name.
  Only devices already paired with the phone are listed; nothing is scanned.
- **It waits before acting** — thirty seconds by default, adjustable from five seconds to
  five minutes. Bluetooth drops at doorways, on a turned wrist, and when a watch restarts
  itself, and nearly all of those come back within seconds. If it reconnects inside the
  window, nothing happens. Both the drop and the stand-down are written into the alert
  trail, so you can see how often it nearly fired.
- The phone is kept awake for the length of that countdown and no longer.

**It cannot tell your wrist from a thief's distance.** Taking the watch off and walking
away from the phone will trigger it. Under "Lock if the strap goes away", off until you
switch it on.

### The buzz says itself twice

Two short and one long, a gap, then again — so it survives being half-missed, which is
what happens when the first half arrives before you have noticed anything.
