## SonderAssist 2.1

### The alert screen can hold its place

On this phone the assistant opens over the lock screen on a long press of the power
button, and it lands on top of the alert — the message goes, the gestures go with it, and
only the alarm carries on. Anything that can clear the alert without the PIN is a way out
for whoever took the phone.

With this turned on, the alert screen treats being covered as a signal: it puts the
display out again and comes back. That is the same lock the detector performs, and it is
the closest an app can get to pressing the power button.

It gives up after five attempts. A fight the phone cannot win should not become a
flickering screen.

**Off by default**, and it needs no new permission — nothing to grant, and nothing for a
restart to switch off again.

It does not stop a very long hold of the power button from restarting the phone. That
happens below Android and no app can prevent it.
