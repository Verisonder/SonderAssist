## SonderAssist 5.4

Shorter text, and more control over the strap.

### The descriptions are gone

Every option explained itself in a paragraph. The longest ran to 339 characters for a
single switch. All 32 of them are now one short line, across the whole app rather than
just the new parts.

The reasoning still exists — it moved into the code, where it belongs. It should never
have been on screen.

### The strap can wait zero seconds

The shortest wait was five seconds. It now goes down to none, firing the moment the
device drops.

### The strap has its own buzz

A separate switch from the grab buzz, on by default. Nothing else about a strap alert is
visible or audible, so this is the only sign you get.

### The strap has its own location delay

Its own slider, nought to ten minutes, separate from the one a grab uses. A Bluetooth drop
and a phone leaving your hand are not the same level of certainty and should not share a
countdown. Needs the location report switched on, as before.

No sensitivity thresholds changed. None have changed since 4.4.
