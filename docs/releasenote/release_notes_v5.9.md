## SonderAssist 5.9

Teach it the difference between a theft and a false alarm.

A new **Learning** section on the home screen. Turn on **Ask after each alert** and every grab
alert is saved with how it fired: which detector, the numbers that tripped it, your settings,
and the few seconds of motion around it.

After you unlock, a notification asks **Was it a theft?** — tap **Theft** or **False alarm**.
Missed it? The home screen asks too.

**Share** sends every saved alert, with your answers, as one text file. Collect enough and
it shows exactly what sets off the false alarms, so the detector can be tuned against them.

Off by default. Nothing leaves the phone unless you share it.
