## SonderAssist 8.5

**F.R.I.D.A.Y**
- The F.R.I.D.A.Y clip plays once per alert. Saying the word again keeps it listening
  without replaying it.
- Once you have said the word, a bare "10 seconds" (or 30, "a minute") is enough.
- A countdown command is answered with "OK boss" or "OK sir", at random.
- A countdown wheel now appears on the F.R.I.D.A.Y screen, counting down to the siren.
  It reads ALERT IN above the number and ALERT at zero; both can be changed under When
  it fires.
