## SonderAssist 1.5

### J.A.R.V.I.S mode

A different alert screen, and a different way out of it.

Turned on, the screen that appears after a lock has nothing on it — no message, no
picture, nothing to read. Touching it brings up a set of turning rings under each finger,
with the dot field brightening beneath them. **Two fingers tapped once arms it; two
fingers swiped up clears the screen and stops the sound.**

The mode brings its own sound and plays it once, so the sound picker and the repeat count
step aside while it is on. The delay before the sound stays exactly as it was: the screen
locks immediately, the noise waits, and that wait is the reason the detector is allowed to
fire on thin evidence in the first place.

Unlocking still dismisses the screen, and still does it faster. Worth knowing before
turning this on: the gesture is not a PIN and the phone cannot check who made it. Anyone
who knows it can clear the screen without unlocking.

### The app can now show you why something failed

The app has been recording its last error to a file since the first version, and nothing
ever read that file back. It does now, at the top of the main screen, with a button to
clear it. If something goes wrong, the reason is on the phone rather than in a description
of what it looked like.

### The quick settings tile no longer fails in silence

Starting the watch from the tile could be refused by the system, and the tile had no
answer for that — the tap appeared to do nothing at all. It now records what happened and
opens the app so the reason is in front of you.

### Plainer wording on the sensitivity screen

The figures the slider moves were labelled *jerk*, which is the right name for the
quantity and no help in reading the screen. They are called acceleration now.
