## SonderAssist 7

A new home screen, and control by text.

**Home** now shows the status and six rows: Detection, The strap, When it fires, Find my
phone, Power menu, Keep it running. Each row says what is on. Tap one to open it; back
returns home. Nothing was changed inside, only where it lives.

**Control by text** (under Find my phone, off by default). Pick a secret word, then text
your phone from any other phone:

- `WORD locate` — replies with position, battery and a map link
- `WORD ring` — loud alarm for 30 seconds, even on silent
- `WORD lock` — locks the screen
- `WORD help` — lists the commands

Anyone who knows the word can use it, so pick one nobody will guess. Allow SMS for
SonderAssist in the phone's permissions, or nothing will be answered.
