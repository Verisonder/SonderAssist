## SonderAssist 1.1

### A quick settings tile

Pull down the panel, tap **Edit**, and drag SonderAssist in. Tapping the tile starts or
stops the watch; holding it opens the app.

Arming and disarming is the thing done most often, and it is done in the moments where
opening an app is least convenient — getting on a train, putting the phone away, handing it
to someone. A tile is two gestures from anywhere.

The tile reads the service directly rather than a stored flag, so it cannot claim to be off
while it is running. If the permission to lock the screen has not been given yet, tapping
the tile opens the app instead of doing nothing.

### Everything from 1

If you are coming from 0.1, version 1 is where the app became usable: sensitivity with the
real figures shown, a message and a background picture on the lock screen, a sound of your
choosing that plays a set number of times after a delay you set, a quick settings tile, and
fixes for the app stopping on its own after a reboot.

It still fires on thin evidence on purpose, still cannot tell a theft from any other motion
of the same shape, and its thresholds are still reasoned from physics rather than measured
from recordings. Those remain the honest limits.
