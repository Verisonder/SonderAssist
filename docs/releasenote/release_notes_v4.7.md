## SonderAssist 4.7

Undoes the mistake in 4.6, and gets the buzz through a silent phone.

### 4.6 could stop a real theft being detected

4.6 ignored a grab while something was against the front of the phone, meaning to catch
the phone going into a pocket. The proximity sensor that reads this sits at the top of the
screen — which is exactly where a hand lands when it takes the phone off you. So it read
"covered" during a real theft as readily as inside a pocket, and suppressed it.

Removed. Nothing checks the proximity sensor any more. **Install this one.**

### The pocket, understood properly, and now optional

The phone goes into a pocket **top edge first**, so its top edge points at the ground the
whole way down. Pushing it down the pocket is therefore a push toward its own top edge —
which is precisely what a hand pulling it away looks like. Same direction, same sharpness,
same shape. That is why the sensitivity slider never helped at any setting.

What is different is which way up the phone is. Going into a pocket it is turned over;
being taken off you it is not. Under **"Ignore it while the phone is upside down"** — off
until you switch it on. A phone held flat in an open palm is not affected.

### The buzz now arrives on a silent phone

It went out unclassified in 4.6, and an unclassified vibration from an app running in the
background is dropped under Do Not Disturb — and may not be delivered at all. It now goes
out as an alarm, the same way the siren does, which is why the siren already reaches you on
a silenced phone.

No sensitivity thresholds changed in this release. Your slider stays where you left it.
