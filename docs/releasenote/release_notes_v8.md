## SonderAssist 8

**F.R.I.D.A.Y mode** replaces J.A.R.V.I.S mode and is on by default. After a theft the
phone locks and does everything else as before, but stays silent until you ask:

- Say **"Friday"** within a minute of the theft, or text `WORD friday`, and it plays the
  F.R.I.D.A.Y clip.
- Say **"Friday 10"** (or 30, 60, "a minute"), or text `WORD friday 10`, and the siren
  starts at full volume when the countdown ends.
- Unlocking stops everything.

Add your own words under When it fires. They work by voice and by text. Allow the
microphone for the voice part. The strap can listen too (off by default).

**Control by text**
- Answer anyone, or only contacts you pick.
- Also read RCS messages, from the messaging app's notifications (off by default, needs
  notification access). The reply goes back in the same chat.

**Alerts** (Detection → See every alert): every saved alert with its time and answer,
the movement around it, and its raw data. Answer any of them from the list. Clearing the
log now asks twice.

**Location**: when your location is still being sent, the home screen says so in red,
with a button to stop it. The Telegram pin can now be stopped even after the app was
closed.
