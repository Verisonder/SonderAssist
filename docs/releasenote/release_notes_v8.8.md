## SonderAssist 8.8

**Text commands** (after your secret word)
- `lock` now starts theft mode — lock, location, the F.R.I.D.A.Y screen, listening — and
  plays the F.R.I.D.A.Y clip.
- `locate` waits up to 20 seconds for a fresh position and says how old it is.
- `locate live` keeps sending your position back the way you asked — by SMS or in the same
  RCS chat — for the number of updates set under Find my phone.
- `stop` ends the location from another phone.

**Find ring**: a notification with a Stop button, and the power button stops it too.

**Sound**: a siren now ships with the app and is the default. Choose Siren, Phone's or
Custom under When it fires. It is used for the countdown siren, the theft alarm and the
find ring.

Theft reports now use a fresh position instead of the last one stored.
