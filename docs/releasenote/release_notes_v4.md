## SonderAssist 4

No behaviour changed. Every switch, slider and button does exactly what it did in 3.3.3 —
this is where they live.

### Two screens instead of one

One screen carried everything: the switch that gets looked at daily, and a dozen settings
that get touched once. The daily thing was at the top of a long scroll with everything else
in the way of it.

The first screen now holds the status, what the detector is doing right now, and
sensitivity. Everything else is behind the button in the bottom corner.

### The switch is one tile

A padlock, the word for what it is doing, and a coloured edge with an arrow pointing the
way a press goes. The whole tile is the control rather than a heading, a sentence and a
button underneath it.

### The real icon

The placeholder mark is gone. The new one is drawn inside the safe area every launcher mask
respects, and there is a monochrome version so themed icons on newer Android use the mark
rather than a generic shape.
