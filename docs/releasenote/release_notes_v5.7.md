## SonderAssist 5.7

The strap works with the watch off.

Before, the strap kept the app running, and the app took that to mean it was watching. The
home screen said "watching" and would not stay off, and the tile could not turn the watch on.

Now watching and the strap are separate. With only the strap on, the home screen says "not
watching", the tile shows Off, and the notification says "Strap only".

Turning the watch on takes effect straight away, not after the next unlock.

Removing the lock permission now stops the strap as well, since it cannot lock anything without it.
