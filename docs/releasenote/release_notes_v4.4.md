## SonderAssist 4.4

Fewer locks you did not mean.

### The sensitivity range moved toward caution

On a real phone the eager half of the slider fired on ordinary handling far too often, so it
is gone. What used to be the middle is now the top, what used to be the bottom is now the
middle, and a new cautious half sits below it for when even that is too eager.

| Slider | Acceleration | With a twist | Movement |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100% | 600 | 365 | 6.25 m/s² |
| 50% | 900 | 550 | 9.0 m/s² |
| 0% | 1,200 | 735 | 11.75 m/s² |

Your slider stays where you left it, so the same position is now more cautious than it was.
The figures under the slider show the new values. Takes effect the next time you unlock.
