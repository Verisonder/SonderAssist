## SonderAssist 4.6

The pocket, and a buzz.

### Putting the phone in your pocket no longer locks it

Raising the thresholds twice did not fix this, because it was never a threshold problem.
Pushing the phone into a pocket ends with the pocket — or your arm — stopping it, and a
downward motion stopped is an upward push: the same direction, the same sharpness and the
same shape as a hand pulling the phone away. A firm push clears any number the slider can
reach, and walking off afterwards satisfies the rest of the check.

What tells the two apart is where the phone is. On its way into a pocket it is already
covered when the stop happens. Leaving your hand, it is in the open. So a signal that
arrives while something is against the screen is ignored.

Only at the moment the signal arrives, never afterwards — someone who takes the phone and
pockets it a second later is still caught. Under "Ignore it while the phone is covered",
on by default. A phone with no proximity sensor behaves exactly as before.

**No thresholds changed.** The slider is the same as it was in 4.4, so leave it where you
have it.

### It buzzes when it fires

Three short and one long, the moment the screen locks — before the alarm's grace period is
up, and through a pocket. Under "Buzz when it fires", on by default.
